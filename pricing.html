A ticket is one feature-sized design task: a new flow of up to 5 unique screens (desktop and mobile versions and loading, empty, error and success states don’t count as extra screens; modals, sheets and drawers do; a complex screen like a dashboard counts as 2), changes across up to 5 existing screens, a new component, or a redesign of one complex screen. For example, a 5-screen check-in flow is 1 ticket and a 10-screen onboarding is 2. Bigger requests are split for you before work starts.

Every plan starts with 3 free tickets and no card. You see real designs for your product before you pay.

Yes. Every ticket goes from design to code. After you approve the design, your AI employee builds it in your codebase with your components. A senior designer checks that the built screens match the approved design, then it opens a pull request for your team to review and merge.

Add extra tickets at your plan’s extra-ticket price, or move to a bigger plan. Unused tickets don’t roll over.

In 2–3 days. We use that time to onboard your team: connect your project management tool, Figma and GitHub, and have it learn your design system, existing screens and codebase.

Yes. Monthly plans can be upgraded, downgraded or cancelled before your next billing date. Yearly plans are billed once for 12 months at 10% off.
